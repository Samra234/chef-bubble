Punjabi Kadhi Pakora

Kadhi Pakora features crispy spiced onion gram flour fritters (pakoras) submerged in a simmering sour yogurt and besan curry. Tempered with red chilies, mustard seeds, and curry leaves.

Prep Time ⏱️ 20 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 45 mins
Total Time ⏳ 65 mins
Servings 👥 5 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Medium

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Whisk yogurt, besan, turmeric, chili powder, and water until smooth without lumps. Simmer on low heat for 35 minutes.
2
Mix pakora ingredients with water into thick batter; drop spoonfuls into hot oil and fry golden.
3
Drop hot pakoras into simmering yogurt curry.
4
Heat ghee, sizzle tempering spices, and pour over kadhi.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Use slightly sour yogurt for authentic tangy Kadhi flavor.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve hot with plain boiled Basmati rice.

📦 Storage Instructions

Refrigerate up to 3 days; pakoras soak up gravy making it thicker next day.
