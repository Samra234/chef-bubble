Homemade Cheesy Chicken Fajita Pizza

Freshly baked homemade pizza with a light, airy dough crust, rich tomato pizza sauce, spicy fajita chicken chunks, bell peppers, onions, black olives, and melted stretchy Mozzarella cheese.

Prep Time ⏱️ 30 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 20 mins
Total Time ⏳ 50 mins
Servings 👥 1 large pizza Persons
Difficulty 📊 Medium

🥗 Ingredients

Check items off as you prepare your kitchen station:

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Roll out pizza dough on pan and prick with fork.
2
Spread tomato pizza sauce evenly over base.
3
Generously layer cheese, spiced cooked chicken chunks, peppers, onions, and olives.
4
Bake in preheated oven at 230°C (450°F) for 15-18 minutes until cheese bubbles golden.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Preheating baking pan ensures crisp bottom crust without sogginess.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve with chili flakes and garlic mayo dip.

📦 Storage Instructions

Refrigerate leftovers; reheat in skillet or oven.
