Shahi Shadiyon Wala Chicken Qorma

Traditional Pakistani wedding Qorma is distinguished by its golden brown grainy gravy (danedar) achieved by crushing crisp fried onions into whipped yogurt along with nutmeg, mace, and kewra water. Elegant, rich, and regal.

Prep Time ⏱️ 20 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 35 mins
Total Time ⏳ 55 mins
Servings 👥 5 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Medium

🥗 Ingredients

Check items off as you prepare your kitchen station:

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
In ghee, fry whole spices and chicken until chicken turns light golden.
2
Add ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, coriander powder, turmeric, and salt.
3
Pour whisked yogurt and cook until oil separates.
4
Mix crushed fried onions into the gravy; gravy will instantly turn thick and grainy (danedar).
5
Add 1 cup water, cover, and simmer for 15 mins until chicken is tender.
6
Sprinkle nutmeg-mace powder and kewra water. Cover for 2 minutes before opening.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Crush fried onions with hands or mortar pestle, never blend in electric blender to keep danedar texture.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve with Taftan, Roghani Naan, or Sheermal.

📦 Storage Instructions

Refrigerate up to 3 days.
