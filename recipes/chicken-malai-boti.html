Creamy Restaurant-Style Chicken Malai Boti

Chicken Malai Boti is beloved for its mild, luxurious flavor and velvety tenderness that literally melts on the tongue. Boneless chicken pieces are marinated in a silky blend of heavy cream (malai), thick yogurt, fresh lemon juice, crushed green cardamom, and white pepper. Charred gently on a skillet, grill, or skewers, each bite delivers smoky aroma and creamy, savory satisfaction.

Prep Time ⏱️ 20 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 15 mins
Total Time ⏳ 35 mins
Servings 👥 4 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Easy

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Pat the chicken cubes thoroughly dry with paper towels. Toss with lemon juice, 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, and 1/2 tsp salt; let sit for 15 minutes.
2
In a separate bowl, whisk together the heavy cream, thick yogurt, green chili paste, remaining ginger-garlic paste, white pepper, black pepper, cardamom powder, cumin, cornstarch, and salt until smooth.
3
Add the chicken into the cream marinade and mix well to coat all pieces evenly. Cover and marinate in the refrigerator for at least 2 hours (or overnight).
4
Thread the marinated chicken cubes onto soaked wooden skewers or metal skewers.
5
Heat a grill pan, heavy skillet, or barbecue over medium-high heat with 1 tbsp oil.
6
Cook the skewers for 10-12 minutes, turning every 2-3 minutes and basting generously with melted butter until cooked through and lightly charred at edges.
7
Optionally give a 2-minute charcoal smoke (dhungar) before serving warm with a lemon wedge.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Using white pepper and green chili paste preserves the beautiful light creamy color of the dish while delivering rich warmth without darkening the sauce.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve piping hot with butter garlic parathas, roghni naan, fresh onion rings, and a cool mint dip.

📦 Storage Instructions

Marinated raw chicken keeps for up to 48 hours in the fridge. Cooked boti can be stored in an airtight container for up to 3 days.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Can I make this in an oven or air fryer? ▼
Yes! Bake at 200°C (400°F) for 15 minutes, or air fry at 190°C (375°F) for 10-12 minutes, basting with butter halfway.
❓ How do I ensure chicken breast stays juicy? ▼
The heavy cream and yogurt marinade acts as a natural tenderizer, sealing in natural juices so the breast stays tender.
