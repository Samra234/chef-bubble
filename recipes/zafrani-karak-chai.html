Dhaba-Style Zafrani Karak Chai

Nothing brings people together like a steaming hot pot of Dhaba-Style Zafrani Karak Chai. Chef Bubble's signature recipe slow-boils strong black tea leaves with whole spices—green cardamom pods, cinnamon bark, and freshly bruised ginger root—in a mixture of water and full-cream evaporated milk. Infused with pure saffron threads, this tea boasts a rich amber tint, velvety mouthfeel, and an invigorating aroma.

Prep Time ⏱️ 5 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 12 mins
Total Time ⏳ 17 mins
Servings 👥 4 Cups Persons
Difficulty 📊 Easy

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
In a saucepan, bring water to a rolling boil with crushed cardamom, cinnamon stick, crushed ginger, and saffron threads.
2
Boil for 3 minutes until spices release their fragrance into the water.
3
Add black tea leaves and simmer on low heat for 3 minutes until tea extracts deeply.
4
Pour in full-cream milk and sugar. Bring to a boil on medium flame.
5
When tea rises to the top, reduce heat and pour high using a ladle repeatedly ('phainta') for 3-4 minutes to create a frothy texture.
6
Strain through a tea sieve directly into clay cups (matka) or mugs. Serve piping hot!

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Ladling the tea up and down ('phainta') while simmering incorporates air, creating a velvety micro-foam and enhancing the rich karak tea taste.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve piping hot alongside crispy Aloo Paratha, Samosas, or buttery Bakery Biscuits.

📦 Storage Instructions

Tea is best served fresh. Leftovers can be kept in a thermos flask for up to 3 hours.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What makes tea 'Karak'? ▼
'Karak' means strong and intense. Boiling black tea leaves longer with full-cream milk creates a thick, strong brew.
❓ Can I skip saffron? ▼
Yes, cardamom and ginger alone create a wonderful aromatic chai, though saffron adds a regal aroma.
