Spicy Dhaba Style Beef Keema Matar

Beef Keema Matar is a fast, flavor-packed minced meat dish. Hand-ground minced beef is simmered with onions, garlic, ginger, sweet green peas, and warm spices until rich, dry, and glistening with spicy gravy.

Prep Time ⏱️ 15 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 30 mins
Total Time ⏳ 45 mins
Servings 👥 4 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Easy

🥗 Ingredients

Check items off as you prepare your kitchen station:

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Heat oil in pan; fry onions until golden brown.
2
Add ginger-garlic and minced beef; break up meat clumps and fry on high heat for 7 minutes.
3
Add tomatoes, chili powder, turmeric, coriander, and salt. Cover and cook for 15 mins.
4
Add green peas and 1/4 cup water. Simmer until peas are tender.
5
Finish with garam masala, green chilies, and mint leaves.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Searing mincemeat on high heat evaporates liquid quickly and prevents boiled texture.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve with hot crispy parathas or naan.

📦 Storage Instructions

Refrigerate up to 3 days; makes excellent sandwich fillings next day.
