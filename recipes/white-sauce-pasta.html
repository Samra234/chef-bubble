Creamy Garlic White Sauce Pasta (Alfredo)

Decadent fettuccine or penne pasta smothered in homemade garlic Alfredo white sauce made with butter, heavy cream, parmesan cheese, black pepper, and oregano.

Prep Time ⏱️ 15 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 20 mins
Total Time ⏳ 35 mins
Servings 👥 3 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Easy

🥗 Ingredients

Check items off as you prepare your kitchen station:

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Boil pasta in salted water until al dente; reserve 1/2 cup pasta water.
2
Melt butter in skillet, sauté garlic for 1 minute.
3
Pour in cream and milk; simmer for 3 minutes until smooth.
4
Stir in Parmesan cheese, oregano, black pepper, grilled chicken, and pasta.
5
Toss well, adding reserved pasta water if needed for creamy texture.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Always cook pasta al dente so it absorbs white sauce without turning mushy.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve hot with toasted garlic bread.

📦 Storage Instructions

Reheat with splash of milk to loosen cream sauce.
