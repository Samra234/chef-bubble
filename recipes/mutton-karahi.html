Shinwari Mutton Karahi

Hailing from Pashtun food streets in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Shinwari Mutton Karahi relies on extreme simplicity: tender young mutton, salt, tomatoes, black pepper, and green chilies. No onion, garlic paste, or red powder is added—allowing the natural sweet meat juices and tomato reduction to shine.

Prep Time ⏱️ 15 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 45 mins
Total Time ⏳ 60 mins
Servings 👥 4 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Medium

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
In a wok or karahi, heat ghee. Add mutton and salt; stir fry on high flame for 8 minutes until color turns dark.
2
Cover and cook on medium low heat for 25 minutes in mutton's own juices until tender.
3
Place tomato halves over mutton, cover for 7 mins, then peel off tomato skins.
4
Bhunno (fry on high heat) while mashing tomatoes until gravy turns rich and thick.
5
Throw in coarsely crushed black pepper and green chilies. Simmer for 2 minutes and serve.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Coarsely ground black pepper added at the very end prevents gravy from turning dark while delivering fresh heat.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Eat with hot Peshawari Khameeri Naan.

📦 Storage Instructions

Refrigerate up to 3 days.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Can I cook mutton in pressure cooker first? ▼
Yes, pressure cook mutton for 10 minutes to shorten total time before finishing in the karahi.
