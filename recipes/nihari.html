Authentic Royal Beef Nihari

Nihari is a historic Mughlai dish traditionally slow-simmered overnight. Beef shank (bong) and marrow bones are cooked in a spice gravy containing fennel, dry ginger (sonth), and whole spices. Thickened with roasted wheat flour slurry and topped with spicy ghee tari, ginger juliennes, and lime.

Prep Time ⏱️ 25 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 180 mins
Total Time ⏳ 205 mins
Servings 👥 6 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Hard

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Heat ghee, fry ginger-garlic and beef shank with bones until seared.
2
Add Nihari spice powder and salt; fry for 5 minutes.
3
Pour 6-8 cups water, cover, and simmer on low flame for 3 hours (or 45 mins in pressure cooker) until meat is butter-soft.
4
Dissolve roasted flour in water to make a smooth slurry; pour slowly into stew while stirring continuously.
5
Simmer for 20 minutes as gravy thickens to silky finish.
6
Serve piping hot topped with extra spiced ghee (tari), ginger juliennes, and lemon juice.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Slow cooking over low heat breaks down connective tissue in shank meat into melting tenderness.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve hot with hot Khamiri Naan or Garlic Tandoori Naan.

📦 Storage Instructions

Refrigerate up to 4 days; Nihari flavor intensifies overnight.
