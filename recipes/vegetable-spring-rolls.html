Crispy Chinese Vegetable Spring Rolls

A crunchy Asian appetizer loaded with colorful shredded vegetables stir-fried on high flame with soy sauce, sesame oil, and black pepper wrapped in thin spring roll sheets.

Prep Time ⏱️ 25 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 15 mins
Total Time ⏳ 40 mins
Servings 👥 8 rolls Persons
Difficulty 📊 Easy

🥗 Ingredients

Check items off as you prepare your kitchen station:

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Stir fry shredded vegetables on high heat for 3 minutes with soy sauce, pepper, and salt; cool completely.
2
Place filling on spring roll sheet corner, roll tightly, fold sides, and seal with cornstarch paste.
3
Fry in hot oil until golden crisp.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Cool veggie filling completely before rolling to keep wrappers crisp.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve with sweet chili dip.

📦 Storage Instructions

Freeze wrapped rolls up to 1 month.
