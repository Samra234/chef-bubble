Royal Special Chicken Biryani

Nothing defines South Asian festive dining like a steaming pot of authentic Chicken Biryani. Chef Bubble's Special Biryani recipe blends long-grain aged Basmati rice with succulent chicken marinated in spiced yogurt, golden fried onions (birista), fresh mint, and kewra water. The secret lies in the ancient 'Dum' cooking method—sealing the heavy pot to trap aromatic steam, allowing every grain of rice to absorb the spicy chicken gravy while remaining distinct, fluffy, and aromatic.

Prep Time ⏱️ 30 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 45 mins
Total Time ⏳ 75 mins
Servings 👥 6 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Hard

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Marinate chicken with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, half fried onions, red chili powder, turmeric, biryani masala, and salt for 1 hour.
2
In a large pot, heat ghee/oil. Add whole spices, tomatoes, and marinated chicken. Cook for 15-20 minutes until chicken is tender and gravy thickens.
3
In a separate big pot, boil 3 liters of water with salt, mint leaves, and whole spices. Add soaked Basmati rice and cook until 70% done. Drain rice.
4
In a heavy-bottomed pot, spread half the cooked chicken gravy at the bottom.
5
Layer half the parboiled rice over the chicken, followed by remaining fried onions, chopped mint, cilantro, and saffron milk.
6
Add remaining chicken gravy and top with the second layer of rice. Drizzle melted ghee and kewra water over top.
7
Cover tightly with foil and lid (Dum). Cook on high heat for 5 mins, then lower flame to minimum for 20 mins.
8
Gently fluff rice layers with a wide saucer before serving.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Parboil rice strictly to 70-80% (it should feel firm in center when pinched); it finishes cooking perfectly during the 20-minute Dum steam phase.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve hot with cool cucumber zeera raita, sliced red onions, and fresh plum chutney.

📦 Storage Instructions

Refrigerate up to 3 days. Steam reheat with a sprinkle of water to restore fluffy rice texture.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Which rice is best for Biryani? ▼
Always choose long-grain aged Basmati rice for superior aroma and non-sticky separate grains.
❓ Can I make this biryani less spicy? ▼
Reduce the red chili powder by half and add extra yogurt for a milder, creamier korma base.
