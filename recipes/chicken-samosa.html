Crispy Golden Chicken Samosa

Samosas are the king of tea-time snacks! Flaky pastry filled with seasoned minced chicken, green chilies, and herbs fried to crunchy perfection.

Prep Time ⏱️ 30 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 20 mins
Total Time ⏳ 50 mins
Servings 👥 10 samosas Persons
Difficulty 📊 Medium

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Sauté minced chicken with onions, peas, spices, and green herbs until dry.
2
Fold pastry sheet into cone shape, fill with chicken mixture, seal edges with flour paste.
3
Deep fry in medium-hot oil until golden brown and crispy.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Frying on medium-low oil temperature yields extra flaky crispy crust.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve with tamarind chutney and green mint chutney.

📦 Storage Instructions

Unfried samosas can be frozen for up to 1 month.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Can I air fry samosas? ▼
Yes, brush with oil and air fry at 190°C (375°F) for 12-15 minutes.
