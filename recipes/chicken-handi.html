Creamy Restaurant Style Chicken Handi

Chicken Handi gets its name from traditional clay pots ('handi') where curries are slowly simmered. This restaurant favorite features bite-sized boneless chicken cooked in a smooth, silky gravy enriched with butter, cream, and dry fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi). It is mild, buttery, and exceptionally velvety.

Prep Time ⏱️ 15 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 25 mins
Total Time ⏳ 40 mins
Servings 👥 4 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Easy

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Heat oil and butter in a handi or deep pot. Fry pureed onion until pale golden.
2
Add ginger-garlic paste and chicken cubes; sauté until chicken turns white.
3
Pour in tomato puree, cumin, coriander, white pepper, and salt. Cook until oil separates.
4
Reduce heat to low and fold in heavy cream and crushed Kasuri Methi.
5
Simmer for 5 minutes until gravy becomes glossy and smooth.
6
Top with sliced green chilies and butter knob before serving.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Rub kasuri methi between your palms before dropping into curry to release maximum aroma.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Ideal with butter naan or sheermaal.

📦 Storage Instructions

Keep refrigerated up to 2 days; warm gently on low heat.
