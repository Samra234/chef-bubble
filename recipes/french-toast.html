Classic Cinnamon Butter French Toast

Golden fluffy French Toast slices made with brioche or thick milk bread soaked in eggs, milk, vanilla extract, and warm cinnamon pan-seared in rich butter.

Prep Time ⏱️ 10 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 10 mins
Total Time ⏳ 20 mins
Servings 👥 2 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Easy

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Whisk eggs, milk, sugar, cinnamon, and vanilla in shallow bowl.
2
Dip bread slices into custard mixture for 10 seconds per side.
3
Melt butter on skillet over medium heat.
4
Fry bread for 3-4 minutes per side until golden brown.
5
Serve warm drizzled with syrup.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Day-old bread absorbs egg mixture better without getting soggy.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve with fresh strawberries and maple syrup.

📦 Storage Instructions

Best enjoyed freshly cooked.
