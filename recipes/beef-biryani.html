Karachi Style Spicy Beef Biryani

Famous in Karachi street kitchens, Beef Biryani is celebrated for its tender chunks of beef, spiced potatoes, and dried sour plums that deliver a punchy sour-and-spicy balance. The beef is slow-cooked until melt-in-your-mouth soft before layering with steamed rice. The combination of rich beef broth and tangy tomatoes absorbed into every grain of rice creates a unforgettable feast.

Prep Time ⏱️ 35 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 60 mins
Total Time ⏳ 95 mins
Servings 👥 6 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Hard

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Pressure cook beef with ginger-garlic paste, salt, and water for 25 minutes until 90% tender.
2
In a large pot, fry tomatoes, yogurt, dried plums, potatoes, and spices in oil until oil separates.
3
Add cooked beef with its rich broth into the masala; simmer until potatoes are tender and thick qorma forms.
4
Boil basmati rice in salted water with whole spices until 75% cooked. Drain.
5
Layer beef qorma and rice in a heavy pot. Sprinkle fried onions, yellow food color, and kewra water.
6
Seal with foil and lid. Steam (Dum) for 25 minutes on lowest heat setting.
7
Mix gently from the sides and serve hot.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Boiling potatoes directly in the beef qorma allows them to soak up rich spicy beef broth.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Pair with chilled kachumber salad and mint raita.

📦 Storage Instructions

Keeps well in fridge for 3 days; flavor often tastes even richer the next day.
