Smoky Charcoal Chicken Tikka

Chicken Tikka is a classic barbecue staple loved worldwide. Chef Bubble's authentic formula marinates quarter chicken legs in tangy yogurt, Kashmiri red chili for natural ruby color, raw papaya for tenderness, and garlic-ginger paste. Skewered and grilled over hot coals or oven-broiled with coal smoke infusion, the skin gets crisp with charred edges while the meat inside remains piping hot and dripping juicy.

Prep Time ⏱️ 20 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 25 mins
Total Time ⏳ 45 mins
Servings 👥 4 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Medium

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Make deep diagonal cuts across chicken leg quarters.
2
Whisk yogurt with lemon juice, ginger-garlic, Kashmiri chili, spices, oil, and salt to form a vibrant paste.
3
Rub marinade thoroughly into all chicken slits. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours (overnight preferred).
4
Grill over outdoor charcoal grill OR bake at 220°C (425°F) for 25 minutes, turning midway and basting with butter.
5
For smoke infusion: place cooked chicken in a pot, put a small foil cup with burning hot charcoal inside, drop butter on charcoal, and cover tightly for 3 minutes.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Longer marination is key to soft meat fibers; hung yogurt prevents watery coating.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve on a bed of sliced rings of onions with green chili mint chutney and lemon wedges.

📦 Storage Instructions

Refrigerate up to 2 days or freeze marinated chicken for up to 1 month.
