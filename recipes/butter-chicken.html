Classic Murgh Makhani (Butter Chicken)

Murgh Makhani is globally renowned for its silky texture and rich taste. Tandoori marinated chicken pieces are pre-grilled and bathed in a pureed tomato, cashew, and butter sauce sweetened with a touch of honey. Balanced with gentle spices and fresh cream, it offers pure comfort food perfection.

Prep Time ⏱️ 20 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 30 mins
Total Time ⏳ 50 mins
Servings 👥 4 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Medium

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Marinate chicken cubes in yogurt, chili, and garlic paste for 30 mins; pan grill until browned.
2
Blend soaked cashews with pureed tomatoes until completely smooth.
3
Melt butter in saucepan, add ginger-garlic paste, then pour in smooth cashew-tomato puree.
4
Add chili powder, salt, and honey. Simmer for 10 minutes until sauce thickens.
5
Add browned chicken, cream, and Kasuri Methi. Cook on low heat for 8 minutes.
6
Finish with a swirl of cream and extra butter.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Straining the tomato-cashew puree through a fine wire mesh sieve guarantees 100% silkiness.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve with hot buttered Garlic Naan.

📦 Storage Instructions

Refrigerate up to 3 days; freezes well for up to 1 month.
