Desi Chinese Chicken Chow Mein Noodles

Street style Indo-Chinese Chow Mein noodles flash stir-fried on high heat with sliced chicken, crunchy vegetables, dark soy sauce, chili sauce, and sesame oil.

Prep Time ⏱️ 15 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 15 mins
Total Time ⏳ 30 mins
Servings 👥 3 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Easy

🥗 Ingredients

Check items off as you prepare your kitchen station:

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Stir fry chicken strips in hot oil until cooked.
2
Add garlic and shredded vegetables; toss on high flame for 2 minutes.
3
Add boiled noodles, soy sauce, chili sauce, vinegar, black pepper, and salt.
4
Toss vigorously on high heat for 3 minutes and serve hot.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Cooking on high flame yields authentic wok hei smoky flavor.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve hot with hot sauce or chili vinegar.

📦 Storage Instructions

Refrigerate up to 2 days.

❓ How to keep noodles non-sticky? ▼
