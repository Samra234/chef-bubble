Zafrani Badami Shahi Rice Kheer

Creamy traditional South Asian rice pudding simmered until thick, flavored with crushed cardamom, saffron, kewra water, slivered almonds, and green pistachios.

Prep Time ⏱️ 15 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 45 mins
Total Time ⏳ 60 mins
Servings 👥 6 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Easy

🥗 Ingredients

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Boil milk in thick bottom pot; add ground soaked rice.
2
Simmer on low heat for 40 minutes while stirring frequently until milk reduces and thickens.
3
Add sugar, nuts, cardamom, and saffron; cook for 10 more minutes.
4
Cool completely and chill in refrigerator before serving.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Serve chilled in clay bowls (matki) for traditional flavor.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Garnish with silver leaf (vark) and slivered almonds.

📦 Storage Instructions

Refrigerate up to 4 days.
