Special Shahi Beef Haleem / Khichda

Haleem is a nutritious royal dish made by slow-cooking cracked wheat, barley, four types of lentils, and beef or chicken for hours until meat fibers stretch and blend completely into the grains. Garnished with golden onions, chaat masala, mint, and lemon.

Prep Time ⏱️ 40 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 150 mins
Total Time ⏳ 190 mins
Servings 👥 8 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Hard

🥗 Ingredients

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Soak grains and lentils overnight. Boil until completely soft and blend coarsely.
2
Cook meat with ginger-garlic, spices, and ghee until falling apart tender; shred meat thoroughly.
3
Combine shredded meat and boiled grains in a large pot.
4
Mash continuously with wooden mallet (ghota) for 30 minutes until stretchy smooth paste forms.
5
Pour hot tadka ghee over top and garnish with crispy fried onions.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Using a hand wooden ghota breaks meat fibers into famous stretchy silk texture.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve with fresh lemon wedges, chaat masala, and naan.

📦 Storage Instructions

Freezes exceptionally well up to 1 month.
