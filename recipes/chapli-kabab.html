Traditional Peshawari Chapli Kabab

Hailing from the historic street food markets of Peshawar, Chapli Kabab is celebrated across Pakistan for its distinct crispy lacy edges and juicy spiced interior. Coarsely ground beef is seasoned with tangy dried pomegranate seeds (anardana), cracked coriander seeds, maize flour (makki ka atta), and scrambled eggs for unbeatable tenderness. Each kabab is topped with a fresh tomato slice and pan-fried to deep golden perfection.

Prep Time ⏱️ 20 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 15 mins
Total Time ⏳ 35 mins
Servings 👥 4 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Medium

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
In a wide mixing platter (parath), place the coarse beef mince.
2
Add the crushed anardana, cracked coriander seeds, roasted cumin, chili flakes, ajwain, salt, and ginger-garlic paste.
3
Add the chopped onion, diced tomato, minced green chilies, chopped cilantro, soft scrambled egg, raw beaten egg, and maize flour.
4
Knead the mixture thoroughly with the heel of your hand for 6-8 minutes until well blended and pliable. Let rest for 15 minutes.
5
Heat 1/2 inch of oil or ghee in a wide flat tawa or skillet over medium-high heat.
6
Moisten hands with water. Take a baseball-sized portion of mince, flatten between palms into a large thin patty (approx 5 inches wide), and press a tomato slice firmly into the center.
7
Carefully slide the patty into hot oil. Fry for 4-5 minutes on the first side until deep brown and crispy-edged, then gently flip and fry for 3-4 minutes on the reverse side.
8
Drain on paper towels and dust with chaat masala before serving immediately.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Cracking the whole coriander seeds and anardana coarsely rather than grinding into powder provides the signature crunchy texture and bursts of tartness in every bite.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve piping hot with fresh tandoori naan, sliced raw onions, lemon wedges, and green mint-tamarind chutney.

📦 Storage Instructions

Mix can be prepared and refrigerated up to 24 hours in advance. Always fry fresh just before dining for the crispiest texture.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What is anardana and can I substitute it? ▼
Anardana is dried wild pomegranate seed. If unavailable, substitute with 1 tsp amchur (dry mango powder) or an extra squeeze of fresh lemon juice.
❓ Why do traditional Peshawari recipes include scrambled egg? ▼
The scrambled egg retains pockets of moisture inside the mince during high-heat shallow frying, preventing the kabab from becoming tough.
