Warm Melt-in-Mouth Gulab Jamun

Golden brown sweet dumplings made from khoya (milk solids) or milk powder soft dough, fried on low flame and soaked in fragrant warm cardamom sugar syrup.

Prep Time ⏱️ 20 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 25 mins
Total Time ⏳ 45 mins
Servings 👥 12 balls Persons
Difficulty 📊 Medium

🥗 Ingredients

Check items off as you prepare your kitchen station:

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Boil sugar and water with cardamoms for 8 mins until sticky syrup forms.
2
Knead milk powder, flour, ghee, and milk into smooth dough without cracks.
3
Shape into smooth ball dumplings.
4
Fry on very low heat until dark golden.
5
Soak in warm sugar syrup for 2 hours.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Fry strictly on low flame so center cooks completely without remaining doughy.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve warm with silver leaf and sliced pistachios.

📦 Storage Instructions

Store in syrup in fridge up to 7 days.
