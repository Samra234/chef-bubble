Tadka Daal Chawal with Zeera Rice

Daal Chawal is the ultimate Pakistani home comfort food. Yellow split lentils (Moong and Masoor) are simmered with turmeric and garlic, then finished with sizzling hot ghee tadka infused with sliced garlic, cumin seeds, and whole dry red chilies.

Prep Time ⏱️ 15 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 30 mins
Total Time ⏳ 45 mins
Servings 👥 4 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Easy

🥗 Ingredients

Check items off as you prepare your kitchen station:

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Boil lentils in water with turmeric, chili powder, garlic paste, and salt for 20 mins until soft; whisk well.
2
Cook cumin Basmati rice separately.
3
Prepare Tadka: Heat ghee in small pan, add garlic slices until golden brown, then toss cumin seeds and red dry chilies.
4
Sizzle hot tadka directly into warm lentil pot; cover immediately to seal aroma.
5
Serve over hot Zeera rice with pickle.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Covering the pot right after pouring hot tadka seals garlic-cumin aroma into lentils.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve with mango pickle (achaar), crisp papadum, and onion salad.

📦 Storage Instructions

Refrigerate up to 3 days.
