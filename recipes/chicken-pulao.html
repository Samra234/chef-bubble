Aromatic Yakhni Chicken Pulao

Yakhni Pulao is an elegant, fragrant rice dish where chicken is first boiled with whole spices (coriander seeds, fennel, cardamom, cumin) to create a deeply flavorful broth (yakhni). Basmati rice is then cooked inside this concentrated broth, absorbing every drop of rich chicken flavor without heavy tomatoes or red chili.

Prep Time ⏱️ 20 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 40 mins
Total Time ⏳ 60 mins
Servings 👥 5 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Medium

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Boil chicken with broth ingredients in 6 cups water for 20 mins; strain clear Yakhni broth and set chicken aside.
2
In a main pot, fry sliced onions in ghee until medium brown.
3
Add ginger-garlic paste, chicken pieces, yogurt, and green chilies. Fry for 5 mins.
4
Pour in measured Yakhni broth (approx 5 cups) and bring to a rolling boil.
5
Add soaked rice, adjust salt, and cook on high heat until water reduces.
6
Cover pot tightly and steam (Dum) on low heat for 15 minutes.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Tying fennel seeds and coriander seeds in a cheesecloth bundle (potli) makes straining the broth effortless.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve with zeera mint yogurt and fresh salad.

📦 Storage Instructions

Refrigerate up to 3 days.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What is the difference between Biryani and Pulao? ▼
Biryani is layered with spicy gravy and cooked rice, whereas Pulao cooks rice directly in seasoned chicken broth.
