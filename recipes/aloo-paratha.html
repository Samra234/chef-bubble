Crispy Stuffed Aloo Paratha

A popular breakfast staple! Crisp on the outside and soft inside, stuffed with spiced boiled potatoes, coriander, green chilies, and dry pomegranate seeds (anardana).

Prep Time ⏱️ 20 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 15 mins
Total Time ⏳ 35 mins
Servings 👥 4 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Easy

🥗 Ingredients

Check items off as you prepare your kitchen station:

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Mix mashed potatoes with spices, herbs, and salt.
2
Roll small dough ball into disc, place potato stuffing in center, pinch edges together and flatten.
3
Roll gently into stuffed round paratha.
4
Cook on hot skillet (tawa), apply ghee on both sides, and fry until golden crisp spots form.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Ensure mashed potatoes have no hard lumps to prevent tear while rolling.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve piping hot with fresh dollop of white butter and spicy mango pickle.

📦 Storage Instructions

Best eaten fresh; cooked parathas can be reheated on tawa.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How to prevent paratha from bursting while rolling? ▼
Ensure potato stuffing is cool and finely mashed before stuffing into dough.
