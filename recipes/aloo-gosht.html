Homestyle Aloo Gosht Gravy

Aloo Gosht is the quintessential Pakistani soul food served at family Sunday lunches. Soft potatoes soak up the rich, flavorful meat broth made from tender mutton or beef.

Prep Time ⏱️ 15 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 40 mins
Total Time ⏳ 55 mins
Servings 👥 5 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Easy

🥗 Ingredients

Check items off as you prepare your kitchen station:

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Pressure cook meat with onions, ginger-garlic, tomatoes, red chili, turmeric, salt, and water for 18 mins.
2
Open cooker, add oil and coriander powder, and fry (bhunno) until oil separates.
3
Add potato halves and fry for 3 minutes.
4
Add 3 cups water for gravy, cover and cook until potatoes are tender.
5
Garnish with garam masala and fresh coriander.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Halving potatoes keeps them firm without turning mushy into gravy.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve with plain white boiled rice or hot chapatis.

📦 Storage Instructions

Refrigerate up to 3 days.
