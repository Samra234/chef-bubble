Oven-Baked BBQ Chicken

Chef Bubble's Oven-Baked BBQ Chicken brings the irresistible smoky aroma of outdoor grilling straight to your kitchen oven. Tender chicken pieces are first seasoned with a signature spice rub featuring smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, and brown sugar. As it bakes, the chicken absorbs rich spices while staying extraordinarily juicy inside. In the final minutes, we brush a generous coating of tangy-sweet BBQ sauce that caramelizes under the oven heat, creating sticky, finger-licking goodness that everyone at the dinner table will adore.

Prep Time ⏱️ 15 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 45 mins
Total Time ⏳ 60 mins
Servings 👥 4 Persons
Difficulty 📊 Easy

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Pat the chicken pieces thoroughly dry with paper towels to ensure crispy skin.
2
In a small bowl, combine smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, cayenne pepper, brown sugar, salt, and black pepper.
3
Drizzle olive oil over the chicken, then rub the spice mixture evenly over all sides.
4
Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a baking sheet with foil and place a wire rack on top.
5
Arrange chicken pieces on the wire rack and bake for 30 minutes until skin turns golden.
6
Remove chicken from oven and brush liberally with BBQ sauce on all sides.
7
Return to the oven for another 15 minutes, brushing with additional sauce every 5 minutes until caramelized and internal temperature reaches 75°C (165°F).
8
Garnish with freshly chopped parsley or green onions before serving warm.

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

For extra sticky glaze, turn on the oven broiler during the last 2 minutes, watching closely to avoid burning the brown sugar in the sauce.

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve hot alongside garlic butter cornbread, creamy coleslaw, and seasoned potato wedges.

📦 Storage Instructions

Store leftover chicken in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days. Reheat in the oven at 180°C (350°F) for 10-12 minutes.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Can I use boneless chicken breasts instead? ▼
Yes, boneless chicken breasts can be used, but reduce baking time to 20-25 minutes to prevent drying out.
❓ How do I ensure the skin turns crispy? ▼
Drying the chicken thoroughly with paper towels and using an oven wire rack allows hot air to circulate underneath.
