Special Layered Royal Falooda

Royal Falooda is the ultimate celebration in a glass! Combining vibrant red rose syrup, soaked basil seeds (tukh malanga), soft cornstarch falooda noodles, chilled sweetened milk, and crowned with a scoop of creamy Kulfa ice cream, toasted nuts, and a sweet cherry. Chef Bubble brings this street-food classic straight into your kitchen.

Prep Time ⏱️ 15 mins
Cooking Time 🔥 10 mins
Total Time ⏳ 25 mins
Servings 👥 2 Glasses Persons
Difficulty 📊 Easy

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Soak basil seeds in warm water for 15 minutes until fully swollen and gelatinous.
2
Boil falooda vermicelli according to package instructions, drain, and chill in cold water.
3
Assembly: Take two tall sundae glasses. Add 2 tbsp rose syrup to the bottom of each glass.
4
Layer 2 tablespoons soaked basil seeds over the rose syrup.
5
Add a generous layer of chilled falooda vermicelli.
6
Slowly pour chilled sweetened milk over a spoon so layers remain distinct.
7
Top each glass with a big scoop of Kulfa ice cream.
8
Drizzle extra rose syrup over the ice cream and garnish generously with crushed pistachios, almonds, and a cherry!

💡 Chef Bubble's Secret Tip

Serve with both a long dessert spoon for the ice cream & noodles and a thick straw for drinking the sweet rose milk!

🍽️ Serving Suggestions

Serve chilled immediately after dinner on hot summer evenings.

📦 Storage Instructions

Assemble right before serving. Soaked basil seeds and falooda noodles can be stored in the fridge for up to 2 days.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What are basil seeds (tukh malanga)? ▼
They are black seeds from sweet basil that swell into gel-coated pearls when soaked in water, providing cooling digestive benefits.
❓ Can I use regular wheat vermicelli? ▼
Thin cornstarch or arrowroot falooda noodles are best, but thin cooked wheat seviyan works as a substitute.
